Reviewers receive student names, immutable submitted questions/rubrics, written answers and selected recordings. Private drafts and unused takes are unavailable to reviewers. Assignment/status revocation is rechecked under existing assignment locks and row locks. Decisions are immutable; expected revisions reject conflicting reviews and identical retries do not duplicate decisions.

7. **Written grading.** `written` questions accept nonempty answers within the configured limit, capped at20,000 characters, with bounded rubrics and reviewer feedback. Only authorized human decisions assign points. Client-supplied scores and extra grading fields reject. No subjective automated grading, AI provider, pronunciation evaluator, transcription or future AI hooks were introduced.
