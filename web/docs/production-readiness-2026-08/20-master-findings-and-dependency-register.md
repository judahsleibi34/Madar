## Dependency rules

Hardware does not close P0 by itself. Node B may not become the sole backup. P0 closure precedes workload migration. Google approval does not replace engineering controls, and engineering controls do not replace Google approval. A development implementation moves to `CLOSED` only after exact production identity and operational evidence prove the end state.
