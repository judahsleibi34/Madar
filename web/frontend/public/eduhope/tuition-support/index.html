Create opportunities for others

We believe education should be accessible regardless of financial circumstances. A portion of eligible course sales is intended to support students experiencing difficulty with university tuition. Learn. Grow. Give Back.

Eligibility: four valid, verified direct course sales through your own referral link. Not Level 4, course completion or lesson count. Failed, cancelled, refunded, duplicate, self-referred and invalid sales do not count. Eligibility to apply does not guarantee assistance.
